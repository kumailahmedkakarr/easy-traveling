Questions people ask

Do I need an account?

No. Just choose your seat and fill in your details. Your booking number and phone number are all you need to find or cancel a booking later.

How do I cancel a booking?

Open My trips (or Track booking from the Help menu), enter your booking number and the phone you used, then tap Cancel this booking. The seat becomes free for others.

Why do you ask for my CNIC?

Bus and car operators need the passenger list with CNIC for every journey. It is used only to manage your booking and is visible only to the operator's admin.

What is the pickup pin?

It is an optional point on the map that tells the driver where you will be waiting. Tap the map, or use My location, and drag the pin if you need to adjust it.

Can I book for friends or family?

Yes. Select more than one seat and you will be asked for each passenger's name and CNIC. Your contact number is used for the whole booking.

Is there a service fee?

Bus bookings include a small fixed service fee that is shown in the fare summary before you confirm. 2D car bookings have no service fee.
