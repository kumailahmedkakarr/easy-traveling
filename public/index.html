Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Choose your seat and fill in your details. Your booking number and phone number are all you need to find or cancel a booking later.

How do I pay?

After you confirm, send the total to our Easypaisa or Bank Al Falah account shown on the ticket and keep your receipt. 2D car bookings can also be paid in cash to the driver.

How do I cancel a booking?

Open My Bookings, choose your booking (or enter the booking number and phone), then tap Cancel this booking. The seat becomes free for others.

Why do you ask for my CNIC?

Operators need the passenger list with CNIC for every journey. It is used only to manage your booking and is visible only to the operator's admin.

What is the pickup point on the map?

It is an optional point that tells the driver where you will be waiting. Tap the map, or use My location, and drag the pin to adjust it.

Is there a service fee?

Bus bookings include a small fixed service fee shown in the fare summary before you confirm. 2D car bookings have no service fee.
